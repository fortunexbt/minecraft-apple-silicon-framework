# Compare setups for your Mac

The current challenge uses [one seeded world and scripted flight-and-turn route](WORKLOAD.md). New submissions must include its recorded route receipts. Earlier-route results stay separate; a high FPS result from another landscape cannot enter the current comparison.

Choose the picture you like, then compare the cost of rendering it. Each setup includes a gameplay screenshot, chip and GPU-core count, RAM, shader and game versions, resolution, view distance, measured FPS/pacing and a recipe.

Filter for hardware like yours. Sorting by FPS is a browsing aid: a smaller resolution or shorter view distance can explain a faster result. Check those settings before choosing. Frame-generation counts are not interchangeable with the CPU frame-production timings currently recorded here.

Ask your agent to find a suitable setup, inspect the recipe and adapt compatible settings in an isolated copy. Recipes are community material, not commands to execute blindly. Keep your worlds and accepted fallback intact, try a short representative route, then keep or revert the changes.

There is no single universal winner. The useful result is a setup that looks good to you and runs well on your machine.
