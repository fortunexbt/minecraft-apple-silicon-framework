 CLEAR RULES BEFORE RECORDS

What earns a win?

The same agreed image, rendered more efficiently. Turning down resolution or removing effects is a different configuration, not a win on the fixed-preset track.

  1. One reference workload. A published world, camera route, visual preset and evaluator Mac. Output and internal resolution, view distance and required shader effects stay fixed. Frame generation and dynamic resolution are off.
  2. Matched repeats. Three baseline/candidate pairs per scene, alternating order. The score uses the median paired p95 frame-time ratio in each scene, then takes the lowest scene result. Faster averages alone do not win.
  3. Keep the image and pacing. Review still images and motion. Correct invisible-geometry culling is allowed; missing visible work is not. Reject worse tail latency or stalls under the published tolerances.
  4. Confirm the result. An independent evaluator repeats the workload. Small differences tie. GitHub popularity, subjective taste and self-entered claims never break a tie.

Current status: the reference workload and official replay process are not operational yet. The site accepts unranked showcases; its current validator checks single-pair evidence, not this future ranking process. Measurements are CPU frame-production timings, not displayed or generated FPS.
